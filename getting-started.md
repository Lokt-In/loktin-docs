# Getting Started

LoktIn is a savings platform built on Stellar, that offers various savings options designed to suit each and every user.

## What you need

1. A **Stellar wallet** e.g [Freighter](https://freighter.app).
2. A **funded account**: You'd need XLM for gas, and USDC for savings. For now, LoktIn is on the Stellar testnet. To get test USDC, you have to first add a USDC trustline, then request USDC from the faucet. This can be done from the [Stellar Laboratory](https://lab.stellar.org/account/fund?$=network$id=testnet&label=Testnet&horizonUrl=https:////horizon-testnet.stellar.org&rpcUrl=https:////soroban-testnet.stellar.org&passphrase=Test%20SDF%20Network%20/;%20September%202015;;).

## Step 1: Connect a wallet

1. Ensure that your wallet is set to the testnet network
2. Click the "Connect Wallet" button at the top right corner of your screen.
3. Ensure that your wallet is funded with XLM on the testnet to pay transaction fees.

## Step 2: Pick your first primitive

Each primitive solves a different problem. Pick whichever matches your goal:

- **Behind on bills?** → [Create your first Plan](/how-to/create-plan)
- **Saving for something specific?** → [Set up Target Savings](/how-to/target-savings)
- **Want to lock idle USDC for yield?** → [Lock In USDC](/how-to/lock-in)
- **Want to save automatically every time you spend?** → [Enable Spend & Save](/how-to/spend-save)
